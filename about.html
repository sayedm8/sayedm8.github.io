About Kira

Why Kira exists.

Marwan Ayman Founder & Lead Designer — Doha

I kept watching great work in Doha live and die inside an Instagram grid.

Kira started from one observation: the city's best independents — cafés, bakers, interior studios, garages, gyms — pour everything into their craft, and almost nothing into the one place a customer actually decides to trust them.

So Kira builds both halves: the site, and the story that fills it. One studio designs it, builds it, photographs it, and films it — nothing gets lost between four people who've never met.

Kira launched without waiting for permission. We built full sites for the kinds of businesses we serve — so the first thing a real client sees is finished work, not promises.

Marwan — Founder, Kira
Based in Doha Founder-led Photography & film in-house Arabic & English
The Opportunity in Plain Sight

The work is ready.
The website isn't.

Doha's independent scene is one of the fastest-moving in the Gulf — cafés, restaurants, interior studios, garages, and gyms launch every month, almost all of them starting and staying on Instagram.

That works until it doesn't: DMs get missed, platforms take a cut of every order, and the page that represents your brand looks exactly like everyone else's grid.

Kira builds the site that sits underneath the Instagram page — the place a customer lands when they're actually ready to trust you and buy.
