The Opportunity in Plain Sight

The menu is ready.
The website isn't.

Doha's food and dessert scene is one of the fastest-moving in the Gulf — new dessert makers, home bakers, and cafés launch every month, almost all of them starting and staying on Instagram.

That works until it doesn't: DMs get missed, delivery apps take a cut of every order, and the page that represents your brand looks exactly like everyone else's grid.

Kira builds the site that sits underneath the Instagram page — the place a customer lands when they're actually ready to trust you and order.

Before you ask

Fair questions.

How much does a site cost?

Pricing is flexible right now — we agree the number together, in person or on WhatsApp, before anything starts. Tell us what you need, we scope it in one conversation, and the number you agree to is the number you pay.

How long until it's live?

A Starter One-Pager takes about two weeks, a Full Site four to five. The clock starts once we have your content — menu, prices, and the shoot booked.

Do I need to buy a domain or hosting?

No — we handle the setup as part of the build. Both the domain and the hosting are registered in your name, so you own them from day one.

Can it be in Arabic too?

Bilingual is core to how we build — this very site runs in English and Arabic. The full build includes both languages; the one-pager can add Arabic when you need it.

Who takes the photos?

Tareq, our photography and video partner, shoots on-site at your place — it's included in every build, not an upsell. No stock photos, ever.

I only have Instagram. Is that enough to start?

Yes — that's the point. Your menu and your Instagram page are all we need on day one; send them over on WhatsApp and we take it from there.

What happens after launch?

The first month of tweaks is included. After that you choose: a simple care arrangement, or a full handoff — you're never locked in.
